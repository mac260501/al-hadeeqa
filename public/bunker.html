Common Questions

Everything you need
to know.

Al Hadeeqa Contracting builds underground shelters in Dubai from AED 100,000 for a precast pod (7–8 sqm, 2–4 people) up to AED 1.9M+ for a fortified estate shelter. The most popular tier — the Safe Room — starts from AED 950,000 and accommodates 10–20 people across 50–80 sqm. Final pricing depends on your villa plot and specification, confirmed after a free site assessment.
Al Hadeeqa Contracting completes underground shelter builds in 6–10 weeks from project start. The precast Pod is the fastest — manufactured in 5–7 days and crane-installed in 1–2 days. Poured-in-place shelters take 6–10 weeks including excavation, concrete pour, curing, MEP installation, and fit-out.
Yes. Al Hadeeqa Contracting is a fully UAE-licensed contractor and handles all required municipality permits and approvals as part of the project. Underground shelter construction is permitted for private residential villas in Dubai. We advise clients on the approval process during the free site assessment at no cost.
Precast shelters (Pod and Compact, from AED 100,000) are manufactured at our Ajman facility and crane-installed in 1–2 days. Poured-in-place shelters (Shelter through Fortified, from AED 500,000) are cast directly in the ground with thicker reinforced concrete walls, greater depth, and fully custom layouts — stronger and more adaptable to complex villa plots.
Al Hadeeqa Contracting builds underground shelters at depths ranging from 2.5–3m (Pod tier) to 5–6m (Fortified tier). Depth is determined by your villa's soil conditions, water table, and plot size — all assessed during the free site visit. Deeper shelters offer greater protection and are required for staircase-entry designs.
Al Hadeeqa Contracting builds all underground shelters with full discretion. The build looks like a standard backyard renovation to outside observers. Once complete, there is no visible trace — the hatch or entry is flush-fitted and concealed within your landscaping. Confidentiality is a standard part of every SafeHaven project.
Every SafeHaven shelter includes: reinforced concrete structure, blast-proof hatch with redundant locking, independent air filtration and ventilation, 72-hour power backup system, water storage integration, communication and monitoring setup, and UAE climate-rated materials throughout. Higher tiers include staircase entry, extended autonomy, and larger living areas.
Our engineer visits your villa within 48 hours, evaluates your plot size, soil conditions, water table depth, and entry options. We identify the most suitable shelter tier and any site-specific constraints. You receive a fixed-price written quote after the assessment — no obligation to proceed, no cost.
Al Hadeeqa Contracting currently builds underground shelters for private villas only. Apartment buildings and high-rise structures require fundamentally different structural approaches. If you own a villa in Dubai with a garden or backyard, you are likely a good candidate — the free site assessment will confirm feasibility for your specific property.
Al Hadeeqa Contracting is a UAE-licensed general contractor with 20+ years of residential construction experience, ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certification, and a 50+ person crew.
